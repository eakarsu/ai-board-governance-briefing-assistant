# AI Board Governance Briefing Assistant

Runnable Next.js full-stack app for Board Governance Briefing.

## Workflows

- `/board-packet-builder` - Board Packet Builder (Briefing): Agenda, packet sections, source documents, approvals, and final delivery status.
- `/cyber-risk-brief` - Cyber Risk Brief (Cyber): Security posture, incidents, control gaps, material risks, and board-level decisions.
- `/ai-risk-brief` - AI Risk Brief (AI Governance): AI systems, agent risk, model incidents, policies, and governance recommendations.
- `/financial-exposure` - Financial Exposure (Finance): Revenue risk, spend pressure, forecasts, variance, and decision points.
- `/regulatory-update` - Regulatory Update (Regulatory): New obligations, enforcement timelines, exposure, and executive actions.
- `/operations-kpi-story` - Operations KPI Story (Operations): KPI movement, root causes, hotspots, owners, and operating priorities.
- `/decision-log` - Decision Log (Governance): Board decisions, open questions, required follow-ups, owners, and due dates.
- `/director-qa-prep` - Director Q&A Prep (Briefing): Likely questions, supporting data, risk framing, and executive talking points.
- `/committee-workplans` - Committee Workplans (Committees): Audit, risk, compensation, cyber, and AI committee tasks and materials.
- `/post-meeting-actions` - Post-Meeting Actions (Execution): Action items, owners, deadlines, evidence, and completion tracking.

## Local Run

Provision an administrator explicitly with `ADMIN_EMAIL` and `ADMIN_PASSWORD`, then run the reviewed migration and launcher commands in `RUNBOOK.md`. No credentials are embedded in the application source.
